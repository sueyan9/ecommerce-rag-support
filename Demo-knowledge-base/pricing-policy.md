# Pricing Policy

This demo pricing policy is for testing retrieval behavior only.

## Standard pricing

Each service can have a default price set at the organization level. Branches may apply approved local adjustments when the organization allows branch pricing overrides.

## Discount controls

Front-desk staff cannot create custom discounts above the configured approval threshold. Discount exceptions should be reviewed by a manager.

## Change management

Pricing updates should have an effective date. Existing completed invoices should not be rewritten when future pricing changes are published.
